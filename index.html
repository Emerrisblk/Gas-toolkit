2. Test result

Test at 20–21 mbar (natural gas) after a 7–10 mbar let-by test and 1 minute stabilising, then a 2-minute test. Edition 4 is mandatory from 1 October 2026. Permissible drops, perceptible-movement limits, retest rules and purge volume match Gas Safe Industry Standard Update 133 (Ed. 2, 15 April 2026). Test pressures and pipe and meter volumes come from published guides, not that document, so check them against IGEM/UP/1B Ed. 4.

3. Pipe sizing for gas flow (BS 6891:2015 Annex A)

Add each section of pipework on the path from the meter outlet to one appliance. A section carries the combined load of every appliance beyond it. Check each appliance path separately.

Method: net kW × 1.1 gives gross, converted to flow (38.9 MJ/m³ natural gas, 93.1 MJ/m³ LPG). Flow is rounded up to the next tabulated rate, then loss per metre × (length + fitting allowances from Table A.5). Each section loss is rounded up to 2 decimals as in the standard’s example. Loss per metre is calculated with the Annex A formula and matches Tables A.1–A.4 within 0.4% (checked against the standard’s worked example). Annex A is informative: get accurate loss data from the pipe maker, and add fittings such as regulators and flexible connections from their data. The fitting allowance uses the largest connection size on the fitting, which can be bigger than the section: enter any difference under Other allowance. Up to 35mm only.
